# Xiaoshubao · Before-You-Go Checklist

PE today—are the sports shoes packed? Xiaoshubao builds the day's packing list from a timetable, helping children check items themselves before heading out.

[Open demo](https://drivinggodj.github.io/xiaoshubao/?demo=mon)

## Turn a timetable into a packing list

Add classes and activities from Monday to Sunday and link the items they need. Today combines them into a checklist, so the daily list does not need to be rebuilt from memory.

## Every check brings departure closer

Check books, stationery and activity equipment one by one as progress moves toward fully packed. The library includes 12 common presets plus custom items such as a science book or swimming gear.

## Make consistency visible

Track fully packed streaks and total checks, with milestone badges to encourage a routine. Parents can help edit the timetable while children take charge of everyday checking.

## Try it

Open it on a phone or computer without installing an app. The demo URL fixes the day to Monday to show a complete checklist; remove `?demo=mon` to follow the actual date.
